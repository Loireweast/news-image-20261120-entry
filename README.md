One image summing up the world news of **Friday 20 November 2026**, drawn as a
newspaper front page. The day opened with Cyclone Akvan slamming into India's
east coast, Russia striking Kyiv overnight, and a rare EU–UK summit in Brussels —
with sliding markets, a fresh Houthi attack on Riyadh, new European arms for
Ukraine and a record-breaking GTA 6 launch rounding out a crowded day.

The ticker also notes COP31 hitting its Friday deadline still deadlocked over
fossil fuels, Italy fining Meta €805m, and the US Senate passing an energy bill
62–33. Denmark's approval of the Greenland pact and Kerch Bridge rail traffic
resuming were also reported on the day.

Every story was checked live against multiple sources and dated to 20 November
2026: events from the previous day — the ICJ genocide ruling and the Shenzhen
summit, both delivered on Thursday 19 November — were deliberately left out so
the page shows only Friday's news.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the repository's `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The image itself was drawn in `tools/scene.html`
(pure HTML/CSS/SVG, system fonts, no network) and rendered to PNG at 1024×1024.
